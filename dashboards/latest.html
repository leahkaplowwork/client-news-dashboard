Global childhood immunization coverage inches forward despite conflict and hesitancy

WHO and UNICEF's July 15 WUENIC release shows 90% of infants received at least one DTP dose in 2025 and 85% completed the three-dose series. The warning signal: 13.5 million zero-dose infants, 7.3 million DTP-to-measles dropouts, and measles coverage still well below the 95% outbreak-prevention threshold.

WHO Director-General's opening remarks at the media briefing - 16 July 2026

WHO's Director-General said the DRC/Uganda Bundibugyo Ebola outbreak has reached 2,073 cases and 796 deaths and is outpacing response capacity in DRC. The operational concern is surveillance: more than 80% of new cases are being detected outside known contact lists, and about two-thirds of deaths are occurring in communities.

Uganda begins countdown to end of Ebola outbreak

Uganda entered the 42-day countdown after its last confirmed case tested negative for a second time and was discharged. WHO notes all 836 contacts completed follow-up, but the risk of new importations remains because the event is still a cross-border outbreak with ongoing DRC transmission.

Bundibugyo Virus Disease Outbreak Situational Report July 13, 2026

Africa CDC's Health Knowledge Hub reported 2,031 confirmed cases and 756 deaths across DRC and Uganda as of July 13, with DRC accounting for nearly all cases and deaths. Ituri remained the epicenter, while North Kivu and Haut-Uele showed severe transmission and high case fatality ratios.
